An invitation to comparative inquiry

Developing questions that travel without flattening the differences between places.

This is an illustrative announcement. It does not report a confirmed activity or an active opportunity.

This sample research update considers how a network can support comparison between differently situated projects. A shared question offers a starting point, while the forms of evidence and the relations at work need to remain open to investigation.

Researchers might follow a device, a standard or a cultural object through several places, asking what enables passage at each point. Comparison then concerns the differences between the arrangements encountered and the connections through which those differences become consequential.

This entry illustrates a place for future calls, workshop reports and accounts of collaborative work. It is not an active call for participation.
