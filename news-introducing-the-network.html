Introducing Digital Silk Road

A place for research exchange across infrastructures, mobility and the circulation of ideas.

This is an illustrative announcement. It does not report a confirmed activity or an active opportunity.

This sample announcement introduces Digital Silk Road as an independent research network concerned with the territorial effects of digital connections. Its proposed scope brings together digital infrastructures, the mobility of people and products, and the circulation of ideas.

The website provides a shared place for member profiles, seminar conversations, essays and network updates. These formats make room for different stages of inquiry, from an initial fieldwork question to a developed comparative argument.

The current edition demonstrates how an announcement will appear. Real membership, activities and institutional relationships will be added as the network’s public material is prepared.
