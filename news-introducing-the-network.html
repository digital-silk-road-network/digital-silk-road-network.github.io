Network announcement

Introducing The Digital Silk Roads and Beyond

Our website is now open, bringing together the network’s research agenda, members and publication highlights.

The Digital Silk Roads and Beyond brings digital infrastructures, the mobility of people and products, and the circulation of ideas into a shared inquiry into territorial transformation. Asia and Africa are our initial focus, with the network’s geography following the connections we study.

Our website is now open. It introduces the network’s research agenda and confirmed members, alongside highlights from published research on the Digital Silk Road, the governance of the cybersphere and the everyday technical work that connects China and Kenya.

We welcome researchers across disciplines and regions who share these interests. Visit the Members page to meet the network or register your interest in joining. Dates, speakers and further details of the seminar programme will be announced on the Seminars page.
